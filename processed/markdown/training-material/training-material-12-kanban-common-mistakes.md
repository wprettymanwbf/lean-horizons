Common Kanban Mistakes

Using Kanban but still pushing production based on forecasts instead of demand.

Having too many Kanban signals, which hides problems and excess inventory.

Ignoring defects and allowing them to flow downstream instead of stopping and fixing the root cause.

Failing to adjust Kanban quantities as demand changes.

Moving materials without a Kanban signal.

Producing more than the quantity specified on the Kanban.

Treating Kanban as a scheduling tool instead of a pull system.
