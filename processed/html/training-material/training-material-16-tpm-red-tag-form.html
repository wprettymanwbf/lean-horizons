Printing Instructions for TPM Handout

Red Tag Handouts

  1. Order the tags on page 2 to be printed in color on white card stock. Be sure the paper size is 8 ½” x 11”.
  2. Instruct the printing company (Kinko’s, Staples, etc.) to cut, punch, and perforate the bottom two tags.
