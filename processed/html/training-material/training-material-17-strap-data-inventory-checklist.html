Reference Notes:

  1. Mission Statement Examples:

Defined: Current State: What do we do? Our purpose for being?

Coca-Cola: “To craft the brands and choice of drinks that people love, to refresh them in body & spirit.”

Starbucks: “To inspire and nurture the human spirit – one person, one cup, and one neighborhood at a time.”

  1. Vision Statement Examples:

Defined: Future State: Where are we going?

Amazon: “Our vision is to be earth's most customer-centric company; to build a place where people can come to find and discover anything they might want to buy online.”

Tesla: “To create the most compelling car company of the 21st century by driving the world’s transition to electric vehicles.” 

  1. Major Changes from Last Year Example:

Defined: Provide significant differences in business conditions, competition, technology, markets, etc.

  • Kaizen improvements in our New Product Development process (time to market from 48 months to 12 months) will provide a significant competitive advantage with new product entries
  • New European environmental regulations will require that we redesign our packaging to be eco-friendly, consistent with the new regulations
  • Labor market has become extremely competitive and high employee turnover has major implications to meeting ever-increasing customer demands
  • Include leading indicators that are relevant to your business. For example:
    • Housing starts
    • Inflation projections
    • Consumer confidence
    • etc…
