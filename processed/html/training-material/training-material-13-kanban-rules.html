Kanban Rules

1. Do not produce without a Kanban: No signal = no production.

2. Do not move material without a Kanban: No card = no movement.

3. Only produce the exact quantity specified: Build what’s needed—no more, no less.

4. Send defective products back: Do not pass defects downstream.

5. Reduce the number of Kanban over time: Less inventory = more visibility.

6. Level the workload (Heijunka): Smooth, consistent flow.

Summary

Kanban is a disciplined pull system where production, movement, and replenishment are driven strictly by demand signals.
