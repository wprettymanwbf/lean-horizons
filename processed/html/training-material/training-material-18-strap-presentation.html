What is Strategy? “Operational effectiveness is not strategy.” - Michael E. Porter First, let’s consider what Strategy IS NOT!

However… Operational Excellence (in terms of SQDC*) enables superior execution of the necessary operational imperatives in which to implement strategy. “You must earn the right to grow.” - Danaher Strategic Policy Statement * Safety, Quality, Delivery, Cost

Strategy Defined Strategy is where we roadmap sustainable competitive advantage for profitable, long-term growth, and we must answer the question… “What game are we playing and how are we going to win?”

But Who Defines Winning?  Winning is defined by your key stakeholders  Cannot be a Zero-Sum game  All stakeholders* must win! * Other Stakeholders include Community, Society, Environment, Suppliers, Competition

1 Mission Statement Defined: Current State - What do we do? Our purpose for being? Coca-Cola: “To craft the brands and choice of drinks that people love, to refresh them in body & spirit.” Starbucks: “To inspire and nurture the human spirit – one person, one cup, and one neighborhood at a time.”

Defined: Future State - Where are we going? 2 Vision Statement Amazon: “Our vision is to be earth's most customer-centric company; to build a place where people can come to find and discover anything they might want to buy online.” Tesla: “To create the most compelling car company of the 21st century by driving the world’s transition to electric vehicles.”

3 Major Changes from Last Year Defined: Provide significant differences in business conditions, competition, technology, markets, etc. Kaizen improvements in our New Product Development process (time to market from 48 months to 12 months) will provide a significant competitive advantage with new product entries. New European environmental regulations will require that we redesign our packaging to be eco-friendly, consistent with the new regulations.

10 Competitive Analysis

Key Questions to Consider:  Can you exploit the weaknesses of your competition?  Can you take share by superior Quality & Delivery?  Can you drive price via value-selling efforts?  Does the market understand your competitive strengths?  Do they care? 10 Competitive Analysis

11 Technological Trends Defined: Tech Trends that might impact the future of the business. Competition has developed a lithium battery WIDGET that has wide market appeal. This will require a transition to convert our product line from AC power to a battery-operated platform. Driver navigation capabilities are being developed in smartphones which will make our stand-alone navigation equipment obsolete. Cordless table-top appliances are replacing traditional ACpowered products.

Key Questions to Consider:  Can your product/company be displaced via disruptive technology?  Do you have a way to drive effective technology innovations?  Are you driving an adequate % of new products in your annual sales?  Do you have the technical talent (Engineering and Commercial) to keep up with the latest technical needs of the market? 11 Technological Trends
